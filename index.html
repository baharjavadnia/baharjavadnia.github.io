How I work

From a messy problem to a shipped answer.

I start with the people and the problem: interviews, data and a close look at what exists. Before drawing anything, I agree with the team what success looks like.

  • Product strategyFraming the problem, scoping it into shippable steps and setting the measures that show whether it worked.
  • Research and testingInterviews, usability tests and accessibility reviews that turn opinions into evidence.

Tools and methods

NotionOptimal WorkshopInterviewsSurveysJourney mapsCompetitive analysis

Flows first, then screens. I explore widely, test early with real people and narrow down with the team until the simplest version is clear.

  • Product and app designMobile-first flows, interfaces and interactive prototypes, from onboarding to the screens people use every day.
  • AI and agent experiencesChat interfaces, agent behaviour and the states in between, so AI feels clear and trustworthy.

Tools and methods

FigmaFigJamClaude DesignMazeUXTweakPrototyping

I turn designs into systems: tokens, components and specs. Then I build with AI agents in Claude Code and Codex, write the skills that guide them, and check every detail on the real product.

  • Design systemsTokens, components and documentation that keep a growing product consistent.
  • Brand, websites and launchBrand identities and story-led landing pages, built in Framer or in code.

✦ Where I write my own agent skills.

After launch I look at what changed: funnels, recordings and usability tests. Then the next iteration starts, and the repetitive parts get automated.

  • Analytics and experimentsFunnels, recordings and A/B tests that show what actually changed for people.
  • AutomationAgent workflows that take the repetitive parts of design and delivery off the team's plate.
