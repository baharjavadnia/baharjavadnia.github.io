How I work

From a messy problem to a shipped answer.

Four steps I come back to on every project, big or small.

I start with the people and the problem: interviews, data and a close look at what exists. Before drawing anything, I agree with the team what success looks like.

Tools and methods

InterviewsSurveysAnalyticsJourney mapsCompetitive analysis

Flows first, then screens. I explore widely, test early with real people and narrow down with the team until the simplest version is clear.

Tools and methods

FigmaFigJamClaude DesignMazeUXTweakPrototyping

I turn designs into systems: tokens, components and specs. Then I build with AI agents in Claude Code and Codex, write the skills that guide them, and check every detail on the real product.

Tools and methods

Design systemsFigma variablesClaude CodeCodexAgent skillsDesign QA

After launch I look at what changed: funnels, recordings and usability tests. Then the next iteration starts, and the repetitive parts get automated.
